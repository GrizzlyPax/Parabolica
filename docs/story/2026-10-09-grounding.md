# Story grounding: first instincts (not canon)

*📻 Radio with Pax, Oct 9, 2026 ([Story bible: light grounding session](https://github.com/GrizzlyPax/Parabolica/issues/477)). **First instincts, not canon.** Pax: it's early, the mechanics aren't settled, so this is a lean to build from, not a lock. Only Pax moves anything into [the bible](../STORY_BIBLE.md).*

## TL;DR
- **The sport:** straight motorsport that **runs the world**.
- **The touchstone:** **early-season Blaseball.** The base deck is mostly motorsport with the tiniest tweak.
- **The tone: "gloves on."** The base game stays grounded. **The strangeness comes from which decks you choose to play with:** deck selection is the dial.
- **A card is kit and know-how:** the way the game reflects real motorsport. Later, **sponsorship and superstition packs** could be other ways to get cards.

## Pax's instincts, in his words (lightly edited)

**1. What is the sport, in this world?** *Straight, but it runs the world.* Real motorsport, played dead straight; cities, factories and families are built around it (the 28 deck teams already show this: race day off by law, a town whose gates close at dusk). Nobody asks why it matters so much.

**2. Touchstones.** *Blaseball, but we're early season of Blaseball.* "The base deck is mostly motorsport, and you see the tiniest bit of a tweak."

**3. How strange?** *Gloves on.* "In the future, I want to be able to just check which decks you're playing with, and then that's when you can get crazy. We're playing with gloves on right now."

**4. What is a card, in-world?** *Kit and know-how.* "It's playing motorsport. It's the way we reflect real motorsport." Down the road: "a superstition and a sponsorship are absolutely ways that they can get them. A sponsorship pack could be a thing you somehow get: we got an extra sponsorship pack, which comes with sponsorship-type bonuses and perks, and maybe they have different requirements."

## What this means for writing (Radio's read)
- **Base deck voice = straight motorsport, one small tweak.** Like the 28 teams: a real-sounding team, one odd house rule. No ghosts, no horror, no winking.
- **Strangeness is opt-in and lives in decks.** The Horror deck (parked in [2026-10-08-horror-deck-parked](2026-10-08-horror-deck-parked.md)) and later themed decks are where it gets loud. The base deck never has to carry the weird.
- **Cards stay literal in the base deck:** gear, upgrades, a driver's knack, named the way a team would name them.
- **Your Oct 6 ideas aren't dropped, just quieter for now:** the TV channel, Management's voice and the Severance texture fit a later layer or deck, not the base game. ([2026-10-06 ideation](2026-10-06-ideation.md))
