# Story notes

Brainstorms and working notes for Parabolica's story, newest first. **Nothing here is canon** until Pax moves it into `docs/STORY_BIBLE.md`. Agents: read these for direction and tone, but don't write game content from them without the bible saying so.

- [2026-10-15 — Stamps by post](stamps.md): what the stamp system calls things (#704): **Seal** (permanent) and **Ink** (fades after 3 races); Parcels are **League Issue**, the rare gift **No Return Address**, and the black **Watcher Box** that leaves a **smudge**; the title stamp is the **Postmark**. All 60 stamps and 8 smudges named by their kept `rd-` ids.
- [2026-10-14 — The voice](voice.md): how races sound (#700): the caller is a role that grows with the step (The PA · {State} Radio · The Network), 15 race moments each, and ten Interval broadcasts (barely wrong; rare, only after big moments; never at Locals).
- [2026-10-14 — The venues](tracks.md): the six American track kinds (#370), each at three sizes: your town's dirt track at Locals, a real town's circuit at State, a real landmark at Nationals (Indianapolis, Pikes Peak, Bonneville, Times Square, the Springfield Mile, Atlantic City).
- [2026-10-14 — The 52 looks](states.md): colours for every state, DC and Puerto Rico (#708): primary, secondary, accent and a dark ground each, from the flag where it's distinctive and the state's own thing where it isn't; every state distinct; Arizona deep copper; the single source for `src/data/states.ts`. Wheel-flags come later (Pax, Figma).
- [2026-10-10 — Ace America (the world, story hub)](ace-america.md): **the story's hub doc.** The Watchers found Earth racing cars (nobody else in the galaxy did) and ended war so we'd only race. The slice: a team principal with a local garage climbs **Locals → State → Nationals** (Ace Toledo → Ace Ohio → Ace America; later Globals and Galacticals for Ace Earth and Ace Universe). State size sets difficulty; Nationals is a 50-state elimination. The look is a race program (red, white and blue, gold highlight, the sash as the title). Cards are stamps delivered in Parcels. The 28 deck teams are scrapped.
- [2026-10-10 — Five worlds (proposal)](five-worlds.md): what Parabolica's world could be, five ways (the Circuit, the National Pastime, the Basement Game, Far Circuit, the Almanac Year), each with its own card; Radio's pick is the Circuit played as a 1978 boxed game, with weirder decks as expansion boxes. Lessons from Slay the Spire, Inscryption, Balatro, Wildfrost, Strat-O-Matic, Topps and Galactic Racer.
- [2026-10-10 — Card names](card-names.md): the run's 60 cards, 8 curses and rank-up cards named (kit with a wink), one icon each, by card id; the corner badge (races or DNA) and the "where it applies" circle; archetypes stay in the back end.
- [2026-10-10 — The nemesis](nemesis.md): how the rival team talks (#624): a Pokémon rival in the Paddock, one line per visit; two drivers each roll one of five personalities (Brat, Show-off, Old Hand, Brooder, Spanish speaker), two Show-offs become a "we" double act; boss race lines; the newspaper debrief headline.
- [2026-10-10 — Car names](car-names.md): car names the way real teams name them: people's names, heritage + model years, workshop nicknames, plain chassis codes, and rarely farm + household words (mix 35/20/20/15/10); the sci-fi pool retires.
- [2026-10-09 — Car vehicles + driver portraits](vehicles-portraits.md): the vehicle and portrait icon lists with weights (90% race car / racing helmet), what's held back for themed decks, and the logo-picker swap (f1-car → speed-boat).
- [2026-10-09 — Grounding](2026-10-09-grounding.md): first instincts (not canon): the sport runs the world, early-season Blaseball, "gloves on" with strangeness in opt-in decks, cards = kit and know-how; refreshed six questions; where story attaches in v0.6.
- [2026-10-09 — Name pools](name-pools.md): proposal for driver and league name pools: 16 new name traditions, a hybrid tier, one-word names as a 1% rule, almanac + household absurd words, understated + countryside league names; backstories and quirks parked. **Oct 10 update:** ~80% American names (each half drawn separately), full-silly names 8% → 2%.
