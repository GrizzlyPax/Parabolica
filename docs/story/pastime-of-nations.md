## TL;DR
- **An alternate 1920s Earth, with the 1950s' faith in tomorrow.** In 1908 the world raced from New York to Paris; something up above watched. In 1909 every nation signed **the Accord**: no armies, every quarrel settled on a circuit. Open-wheel racing is the pastime of every nation.
- **The Watchers** are unseen. "We'll learn later." The base deck plays it straight; stranger decks bring them closer.
- **The whole world races; you choose your start.** Three cities are rolled from anywhere on Earth; pick one. Climb **City → Region → Nation → the Worlds** (Eurovision with engines), taking a title at each step.
- **Banner:** the Wheel, gold on midnight blue. Every nation added a wheel to its own flag.
- **Look:** deco gold on black (Pax keeps black backgrounds for reports), trading cards for the cards.

## The world (Radio's draft)
**The Accord of Nations** (Paris, 22 July 1909):
1. No nation shall keep an army, a navy, or an unkind word for its neighbour's mechanics.
2. Every quarrel between nations shall be settled on a circuit, in public, by daylight.
3. Every town may field a team, and every team may race for its town.
4. The race shall be run fair. The Stewards' word is final, and the Stewards' lunch is free.
5. Race Day shall be a holiday in every land.
6. *Somebody is always watching. Race well.* (Not in the draft. It was there when the ink dried.)

**Twenty seasons.** The world counts in seasons; Season 1 is 1909.
| Year | Season | |
|---|---|---|
| 1908 | — | The Great Race, New York to Paris the long way round (a real race). Something watches. |
| 1909 | 1 | The Accord, signed at the finish line. Armies stood down by Christmas; generals become team principals. |
| 1910 | 2 | The first **Parcels**: crates of drawings at the big works, sent by no one. Cars jump forty years in a winter. |
| 1912 | 4 | The first **Worlds**, at **the Parabolica**: a circuit where three borders meet, named for its long last curve. |
| 1914 | 6 | The Summer of Border Races: the war that would have been, settled in eleven races. |
| 1922 | 14 | World radio: one broadcast reaches every nation. |
| 1925 | 17 | The Wheel goes on every flag. |
| 1928 | 20 | **Now: the Speed Age.** Streamlined trains, chrome diners, Motorama fairs. Tomorrow will be faster. Every run starts here. |

**The Watchers.** Never seen. Known by Article VI, the Parcels each winter, and sometimes applause from nowhere after a great race. Papers say "the Watchers"; engineers say "the Customers"; old soldiers say "them upstairs". They want a good race: close, fair, loud. A dull season gets no Parcels. Who they are is left for later.

**How the world runs.**
- **Stewards:** race officials, and now the world's diplomats.
- **Dispute Races:** two nations' champions settle a quarrel in one race.
- **Parcels:** where new kit comes from, officially. Card packs, in-world.
- **Club racing:** every town has a team; the City Cup is as old as the Accord.
- **Race Day:** a holiday everywhere.
- **No betting:** the Accord frowns on it.

**The ladder.** Three starts rolled, pick one; then the **<City> City Cup** (was the Juniors) → **the <Region> Regional** (the Regional) → **the <Nation> Nationals** (the National) → **the Worlds, at the Parabolica** (the Top flight). Titles: Champion of <City> / <Region> / <Nation> / the World.

**The people.**
- **Titans** run the works teams and get the Parcels first.
- **Families** are the dynasties.
- **Garages** are the privateers.
- **Towns** are the regional teams.
- **Old soldiers** are now team principals and crew chiefs.
- **The crowd** is everyone.

The 28 base-deck teams fit as they are: their odd customs read as traditions from the first seasons. Each gets a home nation later.

**Voices.**
- **The World Radio:** transatlantic, every Sunday. "Good afternoon to the world, and to anyone listening further out!"
- **The Almanac:** each city's own edition.
- **The Stewards**, and **your nemesis**.
- **The Watchers** never speak.

**How it gets weirder.**
- **The base deck** is the season as the papers report it: gloves on.
- **Parcel decks** are gifts from upstairs, unlocked as drivers rank up.
- **A Worlds deck** brings Dispute Races and anthems.
- **The parked Horror deck** is "what the Watchers watched before us".

**Glossary.**
- **Accord:** the 1909 treaty.
- **Season:** the year count since the Accord.
- **Watchers:** whoever is up there.
- **Parcels:** the winter crates of drawings and parts.
- **Stewards:** officials and diplomats.
- **Dispute Race:** one race to settle a quarrel between nations.
- **City Cup:** a town's league.
- **The Worlds:** the championship of nations.
- **The Parabolica:** the world circuit and its curve.
- **The Wheel:** the Accord's banner.
- **The Almanac:** each city's racing paper.
- **Old sport:** what everyone calls everyone.

## The six questions (draft answers for the bible)
1. **What is this sport?** Open-wheel racing: the world's only way to settle a quarrel since 1909, and everyone's pastime.
2. **Who are the teams?** Titans' works teams, old families, garage privateers and towns, each with a home nation and an Accord-era custom.
3. **How strange, and where?** A faint hum (Article VI, the Parcels, applause from nowhere). Gloves on in the base deck; stranger decks arrive in Parcels and higher up the ladder.
4. **Who speaks?** The World Radio, each city's Almanac, the Stewards, your nemesis. Never the Watchers.
5. **What are the decks?** The base deck is the season as reported; later decks are Parcels.
6. **What is a card, and a pack?** A card is kit and know-how, printed like a trading card. A pack is a Parcel.
