## How the new pieces fit
### The pageant and the deck (#788, #789; Pax's Oct 17 steers)
- **The sash** (`look/sash.ts`, `Look.tsx` `Sash`): one centre curve still, half the wave and a 40-tall band; the words sit straight on its middle (`sashTextSpan`: the room between the wheels), measured by a hidden probe `<text>` once `document.fonts.ready` and shrunk to fit, never grown. On a curve Playball's joins break; `look.test.ts` pins that no `textPath` is drawn.
- **The ceremony** (`Standings.tsx` when the step is won): `Confetti` (index-based, no RNG; still under reduced motion), `Pennant` (a long name shrinks to end before the taper), the sash with `drape` (lands askew once, `ace-sash-on`), `Racer` in your colour (`--ace-accent` set inline to the garage colour), the honours (`Rosette` over `TitleRibbon`, `heldTitles(where, true)`), then the Postmark struck (picks previewed as gold `Stamp`s) and "the deck that won it" (`teamStamps(facts, you, lastRound)` as `CardFace`s). The finish sheet wears the drape and confetti when `wonTitle` is set.
- **Titles worn:** `RunHeader.whereWords` renders the held title as a ribbon (a paper halo keeps a state's ribbon off a bar of its own colour); `RunLadder` gives a won rung its small sash and the honours; `MainMenu` takes `titles` + `state` in its summary and shows ribbons under the chequer.
- **The pack** (`ParcelPick.tsx`, `.pp` in `Season.css`): brown paper (`--ace-brown`), cream string, the label (tier as its left edge: red / yellow / green / purple) with the copy from docs/story/stamps.md (`League Issue` / `To: garage, town, state` / `From: the League`; `No Return Address` with `From:` blank; `Watcher Box` / `From: the Watchers`), the three flip in turn (`revealed` 0..3, 180 ms apart; reduced motion: at once), the words on a paper strip (`.pp-foot.ace-panel`, so the tokens flip to ink).
- **Your deck** (`Paddock.tsx` `.pk-deck`): `teamStamps` + the Postmarks, as `CardFace size="small"` with the holder's name and `left` (ink races left); tap opens the same detail sheet as a chip.
- **The route** (`ladderView.ts` `RouteNode`, `RunLadder.tsx` `Route`): per race `venue`, `kind`, `place` (0 = none of yours classified), `stage` at Nationals, `last`. A step ahead gets `routeAhead(run, index)`: `seasonCalendar(leagueSeed(run, index), races)` + `leagueContext(run, index)` through `venueForSetup`, which is exactly what `leagueSetup` builds later (the test pins the two equal). The node map: done in ink with your place, the title race gold with a flag, the next pulsing in the accent with a Parcel marker, ahead dashed; kinds under the nodes, the venue and result in the title attribute.

### The driving suite (#791–#794; Pax's A, then A + B + D from the pitch)
- **Garage titles** (`season/ui/teams.ts`): `garageLabels(facts)` → per team `{ name, ribbon? }` (Locals: the name; State: `Ace {town}` with the locals ribbon; Nationals: `Ace {state}` with the state's ribbon, the seats read like badges); `teamNameLabels(facts)` maps data name → label for the race view, threaded as `teamLabels` through `UniversalHeader` → `TimingTower` (`RowContext.teamName`) → `FinishSheet` → the posters. `GarageName.tsx` draws one. **Town pools** (`data/states.ts`): every state's list is ≥ field + 1 real towns; `TOWN_POOL_OCT14` keeps the old lengths so `legacyTownOf` reproduces a schema-5 town. `run.ts`: `RunSetup.town`, `townOf = setup.town ?? legacyTownOf(seed, stateId)`, `dealTown`, `RUN_SCHEMA_VERSION = 6`, `LOADABLE_SCHEMAS = {5, 6}`.
- **The scenes** (`look/venues/scenes.tsx`, `venues.css`): one `VenueScene` per kind (`VENUE_KINDS`): a 600 × 360 box, `path` (closed, start/finish first, clockwise), `pitLane`, `surface { width, fill, centreDash?, planks?, marks? }`, `dust`, `under(ctx)` / `over?(ctx)` (paper, infield, stand, landmark, the state's name); `SCENE_BAND` (44) is the program band on top (`SceneBand`: Official program · the race line · the venue · its kind). `sceneForKind` / `sceneForTrackId` (via `getDeckTrackById(id)?.kind`). `TrackMap` takes `scene`, `sceneCtx`, `scope`, `followId`: the cars ride the scene's path, trail dust by `speedSmooth / vmax`, and size by scope.
- **The scope** (`components/race/types.ts` `RaceScope = 'all' | 'mine' | 'follow'`): state in `WatchMode`; `followId = scope === 'follow' ? focused[0] ?? mine[0] ?? leader : undefined`; the masthead's `.uh-scope` buttons (Mine only when you have cars; Follow names the car).
- **The stand** (`components/race/TrackCamera.tsx`, `.css`; `RaceView` gained `'stand'`): `cameraFrame(drivers, lapLength, followId, fallback)` is pure and tested: `raceDistance` = laps × lapLength + position; the followed car at `CAMERA.followAt` (42 %) of a `CAMERA.window` (20 % of a lap) wide frame; a car within 70 units of one already in a lane takes the next lane back (0–3; pitting = 4); out of frame folds to `aheadOut` / `behindOut`; gaps in seconds at the followed car's pace (10 ticks = 1 s). `ProfileCar` is the program's racer in a garage colour (exported; the posters use it). The kerb and the centre line slide by the followed car's distance; a crowded frame names only the near lanes (plus the followed, yours and the leader). Layout in `look/race.css`: `.frame-stand` is the camera wide on top (`max-height: 46vh`), the tower and the feed under it; pinned under the header on a phone (`WatchMode.css`). Qualifying keeps the map.
- **The posters** (`components/race/MomentPoster.tsx`, `.css`): `posterFor(type, title, message, driver?, mine?)` turns a notification into `{ moment, head, sub?, driver?, mine? }` or null (tested); `POSTER_MS` holds, `POSTER_PRIORITY` says which later poster may replace which. `WatchMode.showPoster` runs it from `addNotification` (the first involved driver's colour, number and garage label) and from lights out; off at 10×, never in qualifying. Rendered once in `.bf-map` (absolute, `top: 48%`, `z-index: 4`, above the cut banner). Nine moments on eight drawings; the flag's wears a `Rosette` and `Confetti`.

### The words (#786)
- **Tracks are kinds** (`data/deckTracks.ts`: `kind`; the `name` is the Nationals landmark). `season/venues.ts` names the kind for the step: Locals `{Town} Brickworks` / `Main Street, {Town}` / `{Town} Hill Road` / `{Town} Boards` / `{Town} Flats` / `{Town} Fairgrounds`; State `{City} Brickyard` / `Downtown {City}` / `{City} Hill Climb` / `{City} Board Speedway` or `{State} State Fair Mile` / `{State} Speed Trials`; Nationals, Quick Race and the lab the landmark. The {City} draw is its own stream (`deriveSeed(seed, 'season', 'venues')`), no repeat until every town has had a race. `trackForRound(facts, round)` puts the venue's name on the `Track` and **both** race paths build from it (App's live race, `settleRound`'s headless one): what you watch is what the record says.
- **The Interval** (`feed/interval.ts`): Radio's ten lines with the moment each follows; Steward's odds (`INTERVAL_ODDS`: Locals never; State 8 %, 25 % in the title race; Nationals 25 %, 50 % in the Final, 40 % in the Hundred). One roll at lights out on `deriveSeed(raceSeed, 'interval')`; fires once, after the first listed moment in the second half (the Hundred from lights out). Its own `FeedPersona` `'interval'`, framed (`FeedItem.frame`) by the tune lines; `RaceFeed` draws the frame and `LAP —`. WatchMode calls `interval(moment, vars)` at the title, a fastest lap, a last-lap lead change, rain, a pileup everyone walks from, a quick stop, lights out in the Hundred and the step's last race. Presentation only.

## Traps (this pass)
- **The engine keys teams by `teamName`:** never hand it a label; map names to labels at the screen (`teamNameLabels`), and look a label up by the data name. A poster or a tower row that shows "Ace Akron" is still `Okafor Brothers` underneath.
- **`TOWN_POOL_OCT14` is history, never edited:** it records how long each state's town list was on Oct 14 so a schema-5 run reproduces its town. Append towns to a state's list; never reorder or insert.
- **The scenes' paths are display only:** `TrackMap` maps lap progress onto the scene's path length; nothing in the engine knows the shape. A path must be closed and start at the start/finish line, clockwise, or the cars run the wrong way.
- **The camera's frame is wall-clock free:** `cameraFrame` reads only the drivers; the posters' timers are wall-clock (`setTimeout`), so a paused race still drops its poster after its hold.
- **`.frame-stand` is its own grid**, not `.frame-track` with a camera: the rules in `look/race.css` (≥ 800 px) and `WatchMode.css` (phone) are explicit; add a stand rule in both when the track frame changes.
- **Poster ids are DOM ids:** `pm-dots` is one pattern per page (one poster at a time; the Style tab shows nine and they share it, which is fine for a fill).
- **The sash measures itself:** `Sash` reads `getComputedTextLength` in a layout effect and again on `document.fonts.ready`; in tests (no layout) the fit stays 1. Don't put a `Sash` inside a container that shrinks to fit (a flex column with `align-items: center` and no width): the SVG then falls back to its intrinsic 300 px (the finale's stage sets `.ace-sash-wrap { width: 100% }` for that reason).
- **`Star` is a `<polygon>`:** it must sit inside an `<svg>` (the pack's postmark wraps it).
- **`.pp` is the pack now:** `.pp-head` is gone; anything that styles the old tier border should use the label's edge (`--tier`).
- **The route ahead is a promise:** `routeAhead` must build the calendar exactly as `leagueSetup` does (`seasonCalendar(leagueSeed(run, index), plan.races)`); if the calendar rule changes, change both (the ladder test compares the preview with the promoted step).
- **`#/season/draft` is an alias of the road**, kept so old links and `seasonScreenFor` keep working; the draft screen is gone.
- **`rivalGarages` still draws a logo** for every rival garage though nothing shows it: the draw keeps the seeded names and colours where they are. Don't remove it.
- **The Interval's icon is the vendored `metronome`** (game-icons.net was unreachable from the build session). Swap: one line in `icons/registry.ts` (`'voice.interval'`), one row in docs/ICONS.md, the SVG vendored under `src/assets/icons`; `icons.test.ts` pins the table at 65 rows.
- **`interval` and the pileup check use `isOutOfRace`**; a crash is a pileup only when two or more cars share the tick and none is out.
- **The venue name is on the `Track` object, not in the save.** An old save's recorded digests keep the name they were written with; new rounds get the venue's. Never read `getTrackById(id).name` for a run race: use `venueFor` / `trackForRound`.
- **The finale's `picksDone`** requires every driver to pick exactly `TITLE_STAMP_STATS[step]` stats (it used to be true by default).
- **The Paddock's auto-lock effect** writes `lockedIn` for a save that opened its Parcel before #781 and never locked in; it runs once per round.
- **Playwright from the repo root** (`rc-flow.mjs`, `rc-sweep.mjs`, `rc-race.mjs`, gitignored): the Detail view is `button.uh-tab:has-text("Detail")`; the Parcel is `button.pk-sealed` → `.pp-card` → `.pp-ask-btns .rs-btn`; saves for every moment of a run are in the scratchpad's `audit/saves.json` (rebuilt by `build-saves.ts`).
- **The dead-CSS purge** was rule by rule against the TSX class names, protecting dynamic prefixes (`prefix-${…}`); a class built from two strings at runtime needs a comment naming it, or the next sweep deletes its rule.
