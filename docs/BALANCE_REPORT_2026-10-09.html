The Balance Report

What over 100,000 simulated races say about whether a Season is fun. Findings only. We pick fixes together next.

The short version

Three findings, one bright spot

  1. 1

    The stats aren't equal. A +10% card on Speed is worth about 265 times the same card on Reliability. Seven of the eleven stats barely move a result, so most pack pulls do nothing you'd notice.

  2. 2

    Tracks and weather don't change who wins. The driver best suited to a track's favoured stats wins about as often with the favour as without it.

  3. 3

    The draft picks the champion. The team that drafts the best car wins 30% of titles, and the snake order hands that pick to the 10th draft slot every time. The leader usually takes the lead for good by race 2.

  4. +

    What works: the packs, the 2-of-3 lineup call and your edge over the AI all land where they should.

How I tested

Same seed, one change, compare

Any change to a race re-rolls it: about 70% of winners change from any tweak at all, even one that should do nothing. So "the winner changed" proves nothing. Every number here comes from running the same seeded race with and without one change, many times, and comparing the averages. All races use the real Season build: the shared pool, the quick draft and the live race path.

Finding 1

Eleven stats, four that matter

Give a driver a +10% card and see how many points a race it adds. Speed is in a league of its own. Power is a distant second. Seven stats sit near zero.

Only Speed + Power has any bite, because those are the two stats that decide races today (Finding 1). The other tracks favour stats that barely matter, so even a huge favour can't make anyone strong. Rain isn't in the game yet, so Wet strategy is untested.

Looking ahead · v0.6

What this means for effect cards

  • Effects wait on even stats. "Speed Down 5%" and "Luck Down 5%" mean wildly different things today, so effect sizes only make sense after Finding 1 is fixed.
  • In-race effects are flavour by size. A 2-lap effect covers about an eighth of a 15-lap race, so it can't swing results much. Fine, as long as you can see it (tower icon, feed line).
  • Carried effects are the ones that change a season. An effect that lasts 2 races is a temporary card, and it makes the lineup call bigger: race the shaken driver, or bench him?
  • Card sizes should be set in points, not percent. Once stats are even, each metal should be worth a fixed share: Bronze 1 · Silver 2.5 · Gold 5 · Iridium 7.5, with an Iridium worth about one podium place a race, not a win.
