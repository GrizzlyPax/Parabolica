## The theme in a paragraph
It's 1928, more or less, on an Earth a little brighter than ours. In 1908 something up above watched us race cars from New York to Paris and fell in love: nobody else in the galaxy had ever built a car. They're polite and unseen. They took war, famine and the economy off our plate, and asked only that we race. So now every quarrel on Earth is settled on a circuit, every town has a garage, and America, where it all started, loves it most of all. You're a team principal with a small local garage. Win your town and you're **Ace Toledo**; win your state and you're **Ace Ohio**; win Nationals and you're **Ace America**. The whole country listens on the radio. Somewhere up there, so does everyone else.

## The six questions
1. **What is this sport?** Open-wheel racing. Since 1909, it is the world's only way to settle a quarrel, and it is America's pastime.
2. **Who are the teams?** Local garages. **You name yours.** Everyone else's has a plain local name: a family's, a street's, a landmark's. A team's identity is its **title**, and the title climbs.
3. **How strange, and where?** A faint hum, and it grows as you climb. At Locals the Watchers aren't really watching. At State they sometimes are. At Nationals they lean in. The base game plays it straight; stranger decks come later.
4. **Who speaks?**
   - **The caller grows with the step:** the fairground PA at Locals, the state station at State, the national network at Nationals.
   - Each town's **Almanac** prints the results. The **Stewards** rule.
   - **The Watchers almost never speak.** When they do, it's a global broadcast, and it should feel slightly *wrong* next to everything else.
5. **What are Parcels?** How kit arrives. Most are league issue. A rare one is a **gift from the Watchers**, and those come more often the higher you climb.
6. **What is a card?** A **stamp** on a driver's card or the car's card. Driver cards are real trading cards in this world: kids collect them, and a card gathers stamps over a season.

## The world
- **The Watchers.**
  - Unseen, polite, fond of us. Seen once: a light over Times Square at the start of the 1908 Great Race.
  - They speak only by radio, rarely: **the Interval**.
  - They want a good race. They never explain themselves, and we never explain them.
- **The Accord (1909).** Every nation signed it.
  - No armies; every quarrel settled on a circuit, in public; Race Day a holiday everywhere.
  - Article VI, which nobody wrote: *"Somebody is always watching. Race well."*
- **The map.** Today's countries; the Watchers redrew the borders "as they should be". America is the sport's birthplace: we started it, and others are as good or better.
- **Time stands still** between seasons. The Watchers like it as it is: fifty seasons on, still no jet engine. Modern open-wheel cars race past gaslamps, and nobody finds that strange any more.
- **Celebrity.** Winners become war heroes and movie stars in one: newspapers, radio, a monoculture. Larger-than-life, **but only once they've won.** Everyone starts with the same dream.

## The run, as story
| Step | Who you race | Title | The world around it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Locals** | your town's garages | **Ace Toledo** | dirt tracks, the fairground PA, hand-painted signs. Black and white. The Watchers aren't watching. |
| **State** | every town's Ace in your state | **Ace Ohio** | proper circuits, the state radio station, the state's colours. The Watchers sometimes notice. |
| **Nationals** | every state's Ace (all 50, plus DC and Puerto Rico) | **Ace America** | grandstands, bunting, the national network: Americana. The Watchers lean in. |

- **Your start:** pick any of the **52** (50 states, DC, Puerto Rico) on a map, then name your garage. **The state's size is the difficulty:** "Ace Vermont · 5 towns compete".
- **Media and venues grow with the step:** dirt to asphalt to grandstands; a PA to a station to a network.
- **Win the table, win the title.** Win your state once, never again. No dynasties.
- **Each run is a new season.** "Who's Ace America this year?"
- **The title is your identity.** The sash *is* the title: *"Congratulations, you are now Ace Ohio."* Past titles stay with you as ribbons on the driver card.

## Voice rules
- **American, 1920s, with 1950s optimism.** Plain words, a little transatlantic polish on the radio ("old sport"). Everybody believes tomorrow will be faster.
- **Gloves on.** Straight motorsport, kit and know-how, one small wink. Never horror, never menace.
- **Short.** Titles are tight; card names are kit with a wink ([card-names](story/card-names.md)).
- **Never explain the Watchers.** Hint, don't tell.
- **The Watchers' voice is the only discord:** very polite, slightly too formal, slightly out of time. Rare, and rarer at Locals.
- **Never:** real politics, religion, war as a subject (it's backstory), real people, baseball (it isn't important in this world).
- **Always:** the town, the title, the radio.

## Anchors, by content type
### Garages (teams)
- **Yours:** you name it.
- **Everyone else's:** generic and local, a mix of family names and places: *Kowalski & Sons · Mabel's Garage · Riverside Motor Works · The Corner Pump · Delgado Brothers · Two Bridges Garage.*

### Places
Real American towns, big and small, in all 52. Never invented. Each state has its own colours and wheel-flag ([#708](https://github.com/GrizzlyPax/Parabolica/issues/708), `docs/story/states.md`).

### Tracks
Six American kinds: Board Track, Brickyard, Salt Flats, Fairgrounds, Street Circuit, Hill-climb ([#370](https://github.com/GrizzlyPax/Parabolica/issues/370)). They get grander with the step.

### Drivers
Anyone with a licence, mostly young, mostly American names. Winners get famous.

### Cards (stamps) and Parcels
- **Stamps:** kit with a wink, worn on a driver or the car. Card faces keep a corner badge (races, or the DNA mark) and a "where it applies" circle. System: [#704](https://github.com/GrizzlyPax/Parabolica/issues/704).
- **Parcels:** league issue, or a rare Watcher gift.

### Titles
*Ace <Town> · Ace <State> · Ace America.* On a cream satin sash in Playball script, a gold wheel at each end.

### The caller
- **Locals:** *"Uh, number seven is still going, folks. Hot dogs are a nickel."*
- **State:** *"A fine day at Akron, and the Toledo car is giving the capital something to think about!"*
- **Nationals:** *"Good afternoon, America, and anyone listening further out!"*

### The Interval (the Watchers)
*[The broadcast stops. A short tune. A different voice, very polite.] "We are enjoying the season very much. Congratulations, Ohio. Carry on." [The tune again. The coverage resumes as if nothing happened.]* Rare. It should feel faintly out of place.

## Later (not canon yet)
- **The ladder goes on:** Globals (Ace Earth, on the Parabolica, the circuit around the world) and Galacticals (Ace Universe, against other planets).
- Watcher factions.
- Rivals and the nemesis, once there's multi-season play.
- Stranger decks: Parcel decks, and the parked Horror deck, "what the Watchers watched before us".
