To run the simulation:
  1. Open a terminal in this folder
  2. Run: npm install (first time only)
  3. Run: npm run dev
  4. Open your browser to http://localhost:5173

That's it! You'll see a race simulation with drivers, a track, and controls.

================================================================================
                           WHAT IS THIS?
================================================================================

Parabolica is a racing simulation inspired by Blaseball. It generates:

  - Random drivers with procedural names and backstories
  - Teams (Kingdoms) with unique colors and emojis
  - Cars with power, grip, and reliability stats
  - Tags that give drivers, cars, and teams unique abilities
  - Tracks with dynamic events that change race conditions
  - F1-style tire strategy with pit stops and compound variety
  - Crashes, mechanical failures, and tire blowouts for drama
  - Season mode with multiple races and championship standings

The goal is to create entertaining, unpredictable races where you can see
exactly WHY things are happening.

================================================================================
                     THE BIG PICTURE: HOW A RACE WORKS
================================================================================

Every 100 milliseconds (configurable), the simulation "ticks". On each tick:

  1. EACH DRIVER gets a speed calculation:

     FINAL SPEED = Base Roll x Stats x Car x Tires x Tags x Track Effects

  2. Tire wear accumulates based on driving style and compound
  3. Crash proximity checks occur when drivers are close together
  4. Pit stop decisions are evaluated
  5. Tag triggers are evaluated (overtake, pit_stop, final_lap, etc.)
  6. Track events may trigger based on lap or random chance
  7. The driver's position advances along the track
  8. When the leader finishes, all drivers complete their current lap

================================================================================
                              TAG SYSTEM
================================================================================

Tags are special abilities that can be attached to drivers, cars, or teams.
They add personality and create unique race dynamics.

TAG TYPES:
  - Driver Tags: Personal abilities (e.g., "Lucky Break", "Pressure Proof")
  - Car Tags: Vehicle characteristics (e.g., "Rocket Engine", "Fuel Sipper")
  - Team Tags: Organizational traits (e.g., "Pit Wizards", "Lucky Garage")

TAG TRIGGERS:
  - passive: Always active (applies every tick)
  - race_start: Activates at race beginning
  - overtake: Activates when passing another driver
  - pit_stop: Activates when entering the pits
  - crash_proximity: Activates when near a crash
  - final_lap: Activates on the last lap

TAG EFFECTS:
  - speed_boost: Temporary speed increase
  - speed_penalty: Temporary speed decrease
  - luck_boost: Increase luck stat
  - tire_preserve: Reduce tire wear
  - pit_speed: Faster pit stops
  - crash_resist: Reduce crash probability

Tags are visible in the driver info dropdown during races and documented
in the Compendium.

================================================================================
                            KINGDOM (TEAM) SYSTEM
================================================================================

Drivers are organized into Kingdoms (teams). Each Kingdom has:

  - 2 Drivers who share the same car and team stats
  - A Car with Power, Grip, and Reliability ratings (1-5 stars)
  - Team Stats: Tire Management and Pit Crew quality (1-5 stars)
  - A Team Tag that affects both drivers

DRIVER DIFFERENTIATION:
Within a team, drivers have different marker styles:
  - Driver 1: Lighter variant of team color
  - Driver 2: Darker variant of team color

This makes it easy to distinguish teammates on the track and leaderboard.

================================================================================
                         TIRE SYSTEM
================================================================================

The tire system adds real strategy to races. Drivers must make pit stops
and use different tire compounds during a race.

TIRE COMPOUNDS:
+------------+-----------+-----------+------------+---------------------------+
| COMPOUND   | SPEED     | WEAR RATE | CLIFF AT   | BEHAVIOR                  |
+------------+-----------+-----------+------------+---------------------------+
| SOFT       | +8% bonus | 1.5x      | 70% wear   | Sharp cliff, high blowout |
| (Red)      |           |           |            | risk. Sprint tire.        |
+------------+-----------+-----------+------------+---------------------------+
| MEDIUM     | Baseline  | 1.0x      | 80% wear   | Moderate cliff. Balanced. |
| (Yellow)   |           |           |            |                           |
+------------+-----------+-----------+------------+---------------------------+
| HARD       | -5%       | 0.7x      | 88% wear   | Gentle cliff. Durable.    |
| (White)    |           |           |            |                           |
+------------+-----------+-----------+------------+---------------------------+

DEGRADATION CURVE:
  - 0-40% wear: Full compound performance
  - 40% to critical: Gradual 5% performance loss
  - Past critical: THE CLIFF - compound-specific steep drop
    * Soft: Loses up to 32% speed rapidly
    * Medium: Loses up to 20% speed
    * Hard: Loses up to 12% speed
  - Floor at 60% performance (can't go lower)

TIRE BLOWOUTS:
  - Risk only exists past critical wear threshold
  - Chance scales exponentially as wear approaches 100%
  - Soft tires have highest blowout risk
  - Aggressive (low consistency) drivers have higher risk
  - Blowout = instant DNF

PIT STOPS:
  - Pit entry at 85% track position, exit at 15%
  - Drivers cross start/finish line at 50% pit progress
  - Slow stops: Double duration (low pit crew stat increases risk)
  - Quick stops: Reduced duration (high pit crew stat increases chance)
  - Position tracked during pitting so overtakes happen correctly

================================================================================
                         TRACK EVENT SYSTEM
================================================================================

Each track has a unique event that can change race dynamics:

EVENT TRIGGERS:
  - Lap-based: Activates on specific lap (e.g., lap 5, final lap)
  - Random: Chance to trigger each lap (e.g., 20% per lap)

EVENT EFFECTS:
  - Speed Boost/Penalty: Affects all drivers or specific positions
  - Tire Wear: Increases or decreases degradation rate
  - Crash Risk: Modifies collision probability
  - Pit Speed: Affects pit stop duration

EFFECT DURATION:
  - Instant: Single tick
  - Laps: Persists for N laps
  - Race: Lasts until race end

Examples:
  - "Monsoon Season" - Heavy rain on lap 5, +50% tire wear for 3 laps
  - "Sandstorm" - 25% chance per lap, -10% speed for all
  - "Solar Flare" - Random timing, affects electronics, +20% mechanical failure

                            DRIVER STATS

Each driver has stats that define their racing style:

+----------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
| STAT           | WHAT IT DOES                                               |
+----------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
| Base Speed     | Raw speed multiplier. 1.0 = average. Higher = faster.     |
| (0.85 - 1.15)  | The most straightforward stat.                             |
+----------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
| Consistency    | How reliable they are. 0.8 = very consistent.             |
| (0.3 - 0.8)    | 0.3 = completely unpredictable. Also affects tire wear.   |
+----------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
| Luck           | Magnitude of luck boosts when they proc.                   |
| (0.3 - 0.8)    | A lucky driver can upset the favorites!                    |
+----------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
| Acceleration   | How fast they start from rest and exit corners.            |
| (0.85 - 1.15)  | High accel helps in traffic.                               |
+----------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
| Endurance      | How well they maintain speed over multiple laps.           |
| (0.85 - 1.15)  | High endurance = stays strong late in race.                |
+----------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
| Aggression     | Risk-taking behavior. Higher = more risky/reward.          |
| (0.3 - 0.8)    | Affects tire wear and crash probability.                   |
+----------------+------------------------------------------------------------+

================================================================================
                         CRASH & DNF SYSTEM
================================================================================

CRASH PROXIMITY:
  - When drivers are within 3% of track position, crash risk activates
  - Base crash chance: 0.08% per tick
  - More drivers close together = higher pileup risk

CRASH OUTCOMES:
  - 70% chance: DNF (out of the race entirely)
  - 30% chance: Damaged but continue with 15% speed penalty

MECHANICAL FAILURES:
  - Random chance based on car Reliability stat
  - Low reliability = higher failure risk
  - Always results in DNF

TIRE FAILURES:
  - Only occur past critical wear threshold
  - Probability increases exponentially near 100% wear
  - Aggressive drivers are more prone to blowouts
  - Always results in DNF

================================================================================
                          SEASON MODE
================================================================================

Season mode lets you run a championship across multiple races:

SETUP:
  1. Choose number of races (5-20)
  2. Select kingdoms to participate
  3. Select tracks for the calendar
  4. Optionally pick favorite/rival teams

POINTS SYSTEM:
  Position:  1st  2nd  3rd  4th  5th  6th  7th  8th  9th  10th
  Points:    25   18   15   12   10   8    6    4    2    1
  Fastest Lap: +1 bonus point

STANDINGS:
  - Championship order by total points
  - Tiebreaker: Most wins, then most podiums
  - Track wins, podiums, and DNFs per driver

================================================================================
                         CONFIGURABLE PARAMETERS
================================================================================

All of these can be adjusted in the race config (DEFAULT_CONFIG in RaceEngine.ts):

RACE STRUCTURE:
  - Total Laps: Number of laps to complete (default: varies by track)
  - Lap Length: Distance units per lap (default: varies by track)
  - Tick Interval: Milliseconds between updates (default: 100ms)

SPEED SETTINGS:
  - Base Speed Min: Slowest possible speed per tick (default: 5)
  - Base Speed Max: Fastest possible speed per tick (default: 20)

RANDOMNESS & CHAOS:
  - Chaos Level: How extreme random events are (0-1, default: 0.3)
  - Enable Random Events: Toggle events on/off (default: on)
  - Random Event Chance: Probability per tick (default: 5%)

TIRE/PIT/CRASH:
  - Tire Wear Rate: Base wear per tick (default: 0.02)
  - Pit Stop Duration: Base ticks in pit (default: 40)
  - Base Crash Chance: Crash probability when close (default: 0.0008)
  - Enable Crashes: Toggle crash system (default: on)
  - Enable Tire Wear: Toggle tire wear system (default: on)
