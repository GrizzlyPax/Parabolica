## Traps
- **Race view colour is Apex's** (#381): marbles, track map, charts, tower rows. The old fonts (Barlow, Inter, Inconsolata) still load for the race view; remove them when #381 lands.
- **The no-team purple accent equals the fastest-lap purple.** If that confuses anyone, nudge the shade.
- **Special Elite and Old Standard TT have few weights.** Hierarchy comes from size, capitals and rules, never bold.
- **Every Season screen sets `teamThemeVars(team)` on its root:** your team, or in hotseat whoever's turn it is.
- **Season Mode (Hub, M2):**
  - **PR checks don't run tests.** Run `npm test` and `npx tsc --noEmit` before merging.
  - **Season screens remount per screen** (`key={screen}` in `SeasonScreen`) and check on arrival that they fit the season's phase; without that, Draft → Paddock bounced back.
  - **The Season race lives in `App.tsx`** (shared engine). Leaving the route resets the engine.
  - `src/season/race.test.ts` pins **the race you watch = the race recorded**. Never loosen it.
  - **`DRIVER_STAT_ROLLS` stores min + span on purpose** (1.15 − 0.85 ≠ 0.3 in floating point). Switching to min/max shifts every seeded race and breaks the fingerprint.
  - **Grade points must equal `applyCards`' math;** `grades.test.ts` pins it.
  - **`seasonRoster` passes cards to each racer** for display only (already baked into the stats). The race view needs it.
  - **The Paddock is one table on purpose.** A second table for car and team brings back the column drift Pax flagged.
  - **A gear move into a full slot is two `GearMove` facts** (occupant → locker, then card → slot); the engine refuses moves into a full slot.
  - **Never centre racing numbers with CSS:** Special Elite sets its digits high. Use `RaceNumber` (HTML) or `dy={NUMBER_DY}` (SVG).
  - **On phones `.watch-mode` must clip overflow, not hide it** (PR #500). `overflow: hidden` makes it a scroll box and breaks the pinned header and track. The header publishes its height as `--uh-height`.
  - The phone bottom-sheet driver panel is gone (#500): the panel opens inside the tower.
  - **The race driver panel computes OVR from the race's stats,** which already include cards. Don't add the cards twice.
  - **One card "in hand" at a time** (`Paddock.tsx`): picking up gear works like a pack card, and the pack card wins while it's unplaced.
  - **The old Standings page serves only the finale;** mid-season standings are the Paddock's League tab.
  - **v0.5.1 re-rolled every seeded grid** (names have their own stream now). Old seed-by-seed comparisons (saves, screenshots, probe seeds) no longer match; compare averages.
  - **Reliability's base is 0** after Pass One: a future "Reliability Down" effect needs a floor. Keep the Acceleration dial under ~1.5.
  - **Two tests were re-pointed in v0.5.1, not loosened** (the new physics broke their seed luck): the tag-stack test checks the applied speed modifier (1.21), and the runner override test uses driver 3.
  - **Reliability lives on `mechanical.*`**, the dials the engine reads; the `carStats.reliability*` copy is unused.
  - **Stat column constants live in `RosterTable.tsx`; both league tables are `LeagueTables.tsx`.** Edit them there, not in the Paddock.
  - **Vehicle and portrait icons roll from their own seed stream** and nothing is saved; a test proves stats don't move (#489).
  - **"The package" for pace checks lives in `src/racecontrol/pace.ts`** (`packageScore`), shared by Race Control and `scripts/mechanics-probe.mjs`. Never hard-code weights again.
  - **`seasonTable()` returns plain constructor standings** (`points`; no `total` / `bonus`); `DRIVERS_CHAMPION_BONUS` is gone.
  - **Feed `personaEmoji` holds an icon meaning** (`voice.booth`) or, for The Track, the track's own emoji: render with `<GameIcon name={x} fallback={x} />`.
