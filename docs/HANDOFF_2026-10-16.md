## How the pieces fit
### The run (`src/run/`, docs/RUN.md)
- `RunFacts = { setup, titles, leagues }`: the seed, your state and garage, the titles won, and per step the season's facts (draft picks, lineups, Parcel facts, effects, results). **Everything else is derived**: `seasonFactsFor(run, i)` builds a step's `SeasonSetup` (the field by geography, the carried garage, the `LeagueContext` with `step`, `locale` and, at Nationals, the `format`). Never save a derived setup.
- `STEP_PLAN` 5 / 6 / 4. Locals: 5 rival garages of your town at +0. State: every town's Ace, 5 / 10 / 20 by population, lifted +20 / +27 / +34. Nationals: every other state's Ace (49, or 50 from DC / PR), lifted +40 (`NATIONALS_SHIFT`, data; Steward re-fits it in #629).
- Rookies are dealt (`season/rookies.ts`): level 5 ± a wobble, no traits, American names. Rival garages (`data/garages.ts`): generic names, deck colours that skip one too close to yours (#638), a state's own colour at Nationals.
- `runState(run)` → `league` / `league-won` / `run-won` / `run-lost`. P1 of the step's table takes the title; anything else ends the run. `promote(run, picks)` records the title and its Postmark stamps; `replayRun` re-settles every step from the facts and must equal the record (tested).
- Saves: `parabolica.run.v1` **schema 5** (older runs are archived, not loaded); the meta (`run/meta.ts`) survives version bumps.

### Stamps and Parcels (`src/season/stamps.ts`, docs/RUN.md → Stamps)
- A stamp is a run-deck card (`cards/runDeck.ts`, 60 cards, Radio's names) on a **driver card** (4 slots) or **the car card** (5; car and garage share it). **Traits are permanent; gear is ink** at ×2 strength that fades after 3 races (`GEAR_LIFE`, mileage math, "2 races left" on the roster). A full card scrapes a stamp off for the next.
- **One Parcel before every race, pick 1 of 3**, the metal by your table position. One roll: smudged (10 %: the Watcher Box, a one-race curse rides along), else a Watcher gift (0 / 10 / 15 % by step, legendary odds), else league issue. Rivals get none.
- **Postmarks** (title stamps): +6 levels on a stat per driver, outside the slots, for good; chosen on the Ladder before going up.
- `applyCards` is unchanged; `seasonCardsFor` hands the race build each driver's stamps plus their Postmarks. The fold `teamStamps` reads the one ordered `cardFacts` log; a scrape recorded in round r applies whenever the replaced stamp is present (faded ink never makes an old scrape throw).

### Nationals (`src/season/format.ts`, `src/RaceEngine.ts`, docs/RUN.md → Nationals)
- **Four races.** Race 1 the Hundred: `RaceConfig.lapCuts` (`HUNDRED_CUTS`: when the leader completes laps 1 / 2 / 3, 30 / 20 / 10 states go on). The engine ranks teams by their best-placed running car (laps, then distance) and every car of a team below the line becomes **`'eliminated'`**, a `DriverStatus` beside `dnf` (`isOutOfRace` covers both). No RNG: a race without cuts is byte-identical; the golden fingerprint is untouched. A car that doesn't finish the Hundred (cut or retired) is out; a state with none left is out and the run is lost at once.
- Races 2–4 the Final from zero; race 2 starts as the Hundred finished (`RaceConfig.gridOrder`, no qualifying; App starts the engine itself); ties by wins, then the better finish in the last race. P1 is Ace America.
- `SeasonFormat { hundred: { race, lapCuts }, finalFrom, stages }` on the `LeagueContext`; `aliveAt` reads the Hundred's `finishOrder`; `lapCutsFor` / `gridFor` put the cuts and the grid on the `ScheduledRace`, which `prepareScheduledRace` hands the engine, so the watched race and the headless settle are the same race. The digest keeps `eliminatedDriverIds` + `eliminatedAt` apart from `dnfDriverIds`.
- The view: `TimingTower.window` (top three, your cars with a neighbour, the two cars either side of the cut line, "+N running" folds, one "N out" row); WatchMode turns the engine's `cut` / `eliminated` events into the banner and the feed line.

### The look (`src/look/`, docs/LOOK.md)
- Tokens only (`tokens.css`, `--ace-*`), one theme per step via `[data-step]`: Locals black and white, State the state's colours as accents (`data/states.ts`, all 52 looks), Nationals Americana. `Look.tsx` has the parts (`StepTheme`, `Sash`, `Bunting`, `TitleRibbon`, `StateBar`, `AceButton`, `Stamp`, `Parcel`…); `race.css` dresses the race view under `.ace-race`. The five fonts are stored with the app (`fonts.ts`). Race Control → Style is the live catalogue. `icons/theme.test.ts` fails on a hex in a themed stylesheet.

### Identifiers (`src/season/badges.ts`, #725 / #694)
- Display only, never a race input: entry numbers 1..N at Locals; three-letter town codes at State (both cars share it; clashes take the first letter and the next two consonants); **the state code plus 1 / 2 at Nationals** (`VT1`, `VT2`); Quick Race deals distinct two-letter codes. `leagueForRound` puts them on `RosterDriver.number` (a string), so the tower, the finish sheet and `RaceNumber` show them.

### The voice (`src/feed/commentary.ts`, `src/season/feedVoice.ts`, docs/story/voice.md)
- The caller is a role by step: **The PA**, **{State} Radio**, **The Network**; Radio's line pools verbatim, placeholders filled from `FeedVoice` (your town and garage, each car's identifier, home town at State, home state at Nationals from the badge). `voiceNotification` maps a race notification to a moment.
- Hooks (#762): the lights-out line as a Season race's first tick lands (`WatchMode.lightsOut`), the title line after the flag of the race that wins the step (`titleWon`, App answers with `leagueWon`), and a `'weather'` event from the engine the first tick the sky carries rain (no dry weather does; Wet will). The Interval is Hub's #751.

## Traps (this week's)
- **`'eliminated'` is a status beside `'dnf'`.** Anything new that filters on `status === 'dnf'` should use `isOutOfRace(d)` from `RaceEngine` (the tower, WatchMode's sorts, the finish sheet, the debrief and settle's live snapshot already do). `format.ts`'s `isOut` is a different thing: a team out of the season.
- **Lap cuts run inside `tick()`** right after the lap-history snapshot, on that tick's order; `cutsApplied` resets in `reset()`. A race without `lapCuts` never enters the code; the fingerprint test pins "no cuts, no events".
- **A fixed grid skips qualifying** (`RaceConfig.gridOrder`): App must start the engine itself for that race (it does when the schedule entry carries a grid). The grid only exists once the Hundred's result is in; `ScheduledRace.lapCuts` / `gridOrder` are derived by `leagueForRound`, never saved.
- **`rivalGarages(rng, count, towns?, excludeNames, avoid?, colours?)`:** the draws are the seed's and nothing else's; `avoid` (your colour) and a seat's own colour only change what is worn. Don't add a draw.
- **Stamps:** the fold applies a scrape whenever the replaced stamp is present and validates (room / not-on-card) only in the round the fact was written. Carried ink is re-dated through `CarriedCards.gearDealt`.
- **The feed's hooks fire once:** lights out only if the component first saw tick 0 (a mid-race join stays silent); the title check only on the step's last race, at the winner banner.
- **Season replay:** a scripted State (test helpers) can't be replayed; the Nationals tests replay the Nationals step alone.
- **Playwright from the repo root** (`rc-*.mjs`, gitignored): `executablePath: '/opt/pw-browsers/chromium'`; `page.goBack()` hangs on hash routes, use `history.back()`; every button is an `AceButton`, click by text; the Parcel is `button.pk-sealed` → `.pp-card` → `.pp-ask-btns .rs-btn` (→ `.pp-scrape` on a full card).
- **The preview artifact** needs relative asset paths in `index.html`, every `assets/index-*.js` **and every `assets/index-*.css`** (the font `url(/assets/…)` lines), or it falls back to system fonts; publish with the old hashes set to `null`.
