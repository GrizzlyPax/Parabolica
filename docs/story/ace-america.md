## TL;DR
- **The world:** an alternate 1920s Earth with 1950s optimism. In 1908 the **Watchers** (polite, unseen aliens) noticed us racing cars. Nobody else in the galaxy ever built cars; they all skipped straight to flying. So they took war, famine and the economy off our plate, and now every quarrel is settled on the track. **Earth is the galaxy's favourite show.**
- **The game (the vertical slice):** **Ace America.** You're a team principal with a small local garage. Win your city to become **Ace Toledo**, your state to become **Ace Ohio**, then the nation to become **Ace America.**
- **The tone:** Bioshock Infinite's sunny strangeness, a little Atlas Shrugged, Gatsby's transatlantic "old sport", Koshien's whole-country tournament, Miss America's titles and sashes, with Dungeon Crawler Carl and the Hunger Games in the background. Gloves on: the strangeness is a faint hum.
- **The game's new name (planned):** Pax, Oct 10: *"Ace America honestly sounds like an even better name than Parabolica."* **Domain secured: playaceamerica.com** (aceamerica.com is parked at about $10k; skipped). The rebrand gets scoped with Pit next. The Parabolica lives on as the world circuit.

## The world
**The Watchers.**
- **Why us:** cars are Earth's quaint, charming thing. Everyone else "just flew".
- **One sighting, never repeated:** a light over Times Square at the start of the Great Race (New York to Paris), 12 February 1908.
- **They speak only by radio, and rarely.** It started with broadcasts in their own language, then came polite announcements (working name: **the Interval**, after the tune radio plays between programmes).
- **Polite, not ominous:** "We've handled war, famine and the economy. Please, just race. We love it."
- **Earth is a spectacle** for the galaxy (Dungeon Crawler Carl).
- **Mostly our background lore;** players get it in pieces over time.
- **Later, maybe:** factions. **Purists** keep Earth vintage; **Modernisers** want to update it ("we'll just give you penicillin"). That explains the anachronisms, like modern open-wheel cars in 1928.

**The Accord** (draft): the Watchers' terms, signed by every nation in 1909. No armies; every quarrel settled on a circuit, in public; Race Day a holiday everywhere; and an Article VI nobody wrote: *"Somebody is always watching. Race well."*

**The map.**
- **Today's countries:** the Watchers redrew the borders "as they should be", the way modern folks know them.
- **America is racing's birthplace,** the way it's baseball's: we started it, and other nations are as good or better. We just happened to be standing there when the Watchers came down.

**Time stands still between seasons:** the Watchers like it as it is. Fifty seasons in, still no jet engine. That's a joke we can use.

**Parcels (card packs, in-world).** Most are **standard league issue**. A **rare** one comes from a **sponsor or fan out there** (Hunger Games sponsor gifts, Dungeon Crawler Carl boxes).

**The Parabolica (full game, later).**
- **The world circuit:** one long, slow curve that goes **all the way around the Earth**, the ultimate "long way round" after 1908. The Worlds are raced on it.
- **Built by everyone, at the Watchers' direction:** the disbanded armies laid it, nation by nation, from blueprints in the Parcels. It feels like Robin Hobb's dragons directing people.
- **The Worlds are regional blocs** (North America, South America, Europe, Asia, a Saharan bloc…), not every country, to keep politics out. Around 20 invented bloc flags; no religious symbols.
- **Who qualifies is random, chosen by "something"** (idea: a sealed envelope that arrives in a Parcel).

## The game: Ace America (the slice)
**You.**
- **You're a team principal,** hired to run a local team.
- **A new run:**
  1. Name yourself.
  2. Pick one of **about five starting garages**, rolled one per region, each scrapping for some city's title.
  3. Pick your **colour**.
- **A garage is just a name.** Everyone starts weak; it's a roguelite.

**Six regions** (plain names): the West Coast · the Southwest (Texas, Oklahoma) · the Plains · the Midwest · the Southeast · the Northeast. The Mountain states fold into one of them later. Cities are flavour, drawn from a big pool.

**Three steps: City → State → Nationals.**
| Step | You race for | Title |
|---|---|---|
| City | your city's garages | **Ace Toledo** |
| State | every city's Ace in your state | **Ace Ohio** |
| Nationals | every state's Ace | **Ace America** |

- **Win the table, win the title.** No boss race.
- **Difficulty is geography:** the State field follows population. Each garage card shows **the honest number**, e.g. *"Ace Vermont · 5 cities compete"*. Roughly **5 / 10 / 20 at State = easy / medium / hard.** Big cities field more garages too.
- **The Nationals:** all 50 states start, **100 cars on track.** Race one: anyone who doesn't finish is out. Then Hunger Games-style cuts, the bottom finishers out, down to the **top 10 states (20 cars)** for a normal closing series. The winner is **Ace America**, and the slice ends there.
- **Win your state once, never again** (Miss America). A constant rotation and no dynasties, which is also why every run starts fresh.
- **Each run is a new season:** "Who's Ace America this year? Will it be you?" Not a time loop.
- **Between seasons, later:** some stats carry over, or the deck grows as you hit milestones (Dead Cells).
- **Your identity is your title.** Drivers stay the same; the brand climbs to your best title. You can rebrand or recolour between steps, and past titles stay visible, so anyone can see where you came from. The shorthand is just the place ("Ohio"); the full title is the moment: *"Congratulations, you are now Ace Ohio."*
- **No rivals or nemesis for now:** only one team goes up, so there's no one to follow you. They come back with multi-season play, when you keep meeting teams you've raced before.

## Drivers, fame and cards
- **Drivers are anyone with a licence,** mostly young.
- **Winners become war heroes and movie stars in one,** at the birth of celebrity: newspapers, radio, maybe early TV the Watchers handed over. A monoculture. Larger-than-life, **but only once they've won.** Everyone starts with the same dream.
- **Koshien is the touchstone:** hundreds of teams, single performances, the whole country watching one silly little sport, everyone with one chance.
- **Driver trading cards exist in-world** (cigarette and gum cards): that's *why* the game has cards.
- **Design idea for later:** traits as **stamps on the driver's card**, so the printed card updates as the driver earns them.
- **Later:** regions known for a style ("folks from the Plains always drive like this"); other nations run their circuits their own way (some militaristic; America runs it like baseball).

## The look
- **Era:** the 1920s with the 1950s' faith in tomorrow. Deco sunbursts, gold rules, chrome, streamlined lettering. Reports on **black backgrounds.**
- **Sashes:** drivers wear their titles ("ACE TEXAS").
- **Pennants** for city titles; for states, **that state's flag with a wheel worked in** (50 wheel-flags over time).
- **The Wheel:** the Accord's banner, a gold wheel on midnight blue. America's flag swaps its stars for one wheel; Ace America's prize carries the Wheel.
- **Cards:** trading cards. The rules in [card-names](card-names.md) still hold: a corner badge (races, or the DNA mark), a "where it applies" circle, its own icon, quiet metal.

## Beyond America (later; not in this version)
- **The ladder keeps going: Ace America → Ace Earth → Ace Universe** (Pax, Oct 10). Possibly the surprise when you win the slice: there's more.
- **Ace Earth:** you race the other nations' champions (the regional blocs, on the Parabolica).
- **Ace Universe:** after Earth's success, **other planets have started racing too.** Nobody else in the galaxy ever built cars until they watched us; now they want in, and you race them to be Ace Universe.

## How it gets weirder (later decks)
- **The base deck:** the season as the papers report it, gloves on.
- **Later decks:** Parcel decks (gifts from upstairs), Watcher factions, the Worlds and the Parabolica, and the parked Horror deck ("what the Watchers watched before us").

## What this would change (for Pit, later; nothing yet)
- **The run:** four leagues become **three** (City → State → Nationals). A new start (name, garage, colour) replaces the draft-based start, and the Nationals become a 50-state elimination.
- **Teams:** the **28 base-deck teams are scrapped** (Pax: "I don't care about any of them"). Teams are local garages whose identity is their title.
- **Nemesis and boss races are parked** (the nemesis voice, #624/#635, waits for multi-season play).
- **Ranks:** "Ace" becomes the title word, which clashes with the driver rank Ace (Pax doesn't mind; rename the rank later).
- **Driver names:** mostly American, matching the slice (the Oct 10 call holds).
- **Tracks:** six American kinds (Board Track, Brickyard, Salt Flats, Fairgrounds, Street Circuit, Hill-climb) with local names: a later pass.
- **Cards:** traits as stamps on a driver's card (design, later).
