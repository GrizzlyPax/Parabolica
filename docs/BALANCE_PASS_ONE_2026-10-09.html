What tuning can't fix

The last gap needs catch-up

I tried shrinking the car further to pull the favourite rate into 30–45%. It moves slowly, and the best car's title share hardly moves at all until the car stops mattering, which goes against your 42% call.

Why: over ten races the dice average out (√10 ≈ 3.2× less noise), so a team that's stronger on paper keeps finishing ahead. Extra chaos inside a race (more safety cars, more qualifying flubs) helped, but each race still resets to the same pecking order.

What closes it: catch-up between races. Today only your team opens packs, and the worse your standing, the better the pack. Give every team a pack on the same rule and the teams at the back get stronger as the season goes. That's the CPU Paddock hook already planned for v0.6, and I can measure it once it exists.
