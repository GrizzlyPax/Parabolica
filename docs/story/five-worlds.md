# Five worlds: what is Parabolica? (proposal)

*📻 Radio, Oct 10 2026, while Pax was out on a run. **A proposal, not canon.** Page with the cards and tables: [Five Worlds](https://claude.ai/artifact/QZBSzFg5MEStyg5826jLwV). Pax asked: what is this world (alternate US? another planet? modern, future, fantasy?), what should the cards feel like in your hands, and how do sillier decks arrive later. Touchstones he named: Star Wars: Galactic Racer (after the Empire falls, everybody races: a global circus that travels), loosely baseball, tactile tabletop UIs, Inscryption.*

## TL;DR
- **Five worlds, each with its own card:** A The Circuit (paddock pass) · B The National Pastime (trading card) · C The Basement Game (cardboard chit) · D Far Circuit (steel tag) · E The Almanac Year (seed packet).
- **Radio's pick: A + C.** The world is the Circuit; you play it as a 1978 boxed game on a card table, against someone across from you. Weirder decks are expansion boxes.
- **Every card keeps the same parts** in every world: the corner badge (races or DNA), the "where it applies" circle, its name, its icon, what it does, and quiet metal ([card-names](card-names.md)).

## What other games teach
| Game | Lesson | For us |
|---|---|---|
| Slay the Spire | Frame shape = type, banner colour = rarity, cost in the corner | Corner badge; quiet metal |
| Inscryption | The game is a table; someone across it plays you; icons over text | World C; icons first |
| Balatro | Felt, wobble, lift and snap: physical without 3D; rarity is a small label | Tactility from motion and surfaces |
| Wildfrost | A countdown on every card, always in the same place | Race timer and DNA "keeps" |
| Strat-O-Matic (1962) | A sports sim with player cards and dice, built from real stats | Parabolica is this, with cars |
| Topps baseball cards | Position circle, stats and a cartoon on the back, misprints | "Where it applies" circle; curses as misprints |
| Star Wars: Galactic Racer (Oct 2026) | After the Empire, an unsanctioned league; runs, one token life, a rival champion | Our run and nemesis; World A's hook |

## The five worlds
**A · The Circuit.** *The war ended, and the world went racing.* An Earth with its own map, in a chrome-and-canvas 1950s–60s that never ended. After the Long War (nobody says who won) the nations signed the Parabolica Accord: disputes are settled on the track. Works teams were the old war factories; dynasties kept the peace; regionals host the rounds. The Juniors race county-fair ovals; the Top flight *is* the Circuit, a travelling circus on one long train. The nemesis is another act on the same train. Cards are passes and permits (gear passes punched race by race, traits as licence endorsements, curses as stewards' penalty notices, wild cards as golden tickets). Weirder decks are legs of the Far Tour, to places not on the map. *Watch:* the war is backstory, never the subject.

**B · The National Pastime.** *In 1904, America picked motor racing instead of baseball.* Alternate US, timeless Americana. Every town has a team. Leagues run like baseball's minors (rookie ball → the Show). The nemesis is the cross-county rival. Cards are trading cards with gum; Iridium is a chrome chase card; curses are misprints. Weirder decks are eras of a league office that gets stranger every season (Blaseball). *Watch:* baseball could drown the motorsport; best as flavour.

**C · The Basement Game** (a frame, not a world). *You're not at the race. You're at the table.* 1978: a boxed game called PARABOLICA on a card table, someone across from you (the Commissioner: hands and a ledger, Inscryption-style). A Strat-O-Matic for motorsport; the sim is "the dice". Four league boards, a bigger one unfolded each time you go up. Cards are die-cut chits; pegs count races down; stars print the metal. Weirder decks are **expansion boxes**: Base Set, then Night Races, then the Horror deck in an unmarked box nobody remembers buying. Ranking up rookies unlocks expansions. *Watch:* the opponent stays friendly-strange, never menacing.

**D · Far Circuit.** *Out past the last colony, the race is the law.* A frontier planet called Parabolica; settlements race for water and ore rights under the Founders' Charter. Cards are stamped steel tags. Weirder decks go past the edge of the map. *Watch:* furthest from "gloves on"; sci-fi car names retired Oct 10.

**E · The Almanac Year.** *Every town has its race day, and the almanac knows the winner.* Pastoral market towns; races keep the calendar. Cards are seed packets with fair ribbons; traits are perennials, curses blight notices. Weirder decks come with the seasons (autumn and winter folk horror, where the parked Horror deck lives). *Watch:* quiet; may not carry a fast sport.

## Steers on the page
1. **The world:** A · B · D · E · a mix of A + B (Radio: **A**).
2. **Where you are while you play:** inside the world · at the table (C) · watching TV (Radio: **at the table**).
3. **The card:** pass · trading card · chit · steel tag · seed packet (Radio: **chit**).

## If A + C wins (Radio's read, for later)
- **Screens become surfaces:** the Paddock is the table, packs are envelopes you tear, the league ladder is the board unfolding, the debrief is the Paddock Almanac (the train's paper) left on the table.
- **Voices find seats:** the nemesis talks across the table; Management / the Commissioner keeps the ledger; the Booth is the box's rulebook and a radio in the corner.
- **Deck growth is a story:** each expansion box is stranger; the Horror deck (parked) is the unmarked box.
